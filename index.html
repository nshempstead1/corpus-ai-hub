FAQ

What is the relationship between Corpus and Corpus Localization?

One company. Corpus is the public brand of Corpus Localization LLC, a New Jersey company. Certified translations ship under the Corpus Localization name; software, services, and growth work ship under Corpus. Invoices say Corpus Localization LLC.

Where do I pay?

Subscription products and Applylane check out right here through Stripe. Deadline-file services check out on their own storefront pages, linked from the services page. Translations are quoted and ordered at corpuslocalization.com. Growth work is quoted by email first, then invoiced through Stripe.

Do you guarantee outcomes?

No. We guarantee the deliverable and the deadline: the packet, the report, the page, the alert. Nobody can honestly guarantee what Google, Amazon, a court, or an ad auction decides, and we do not.

Do you take logins?

Service lines never take Google, Amazon, or USCIS logins. Watch Desk's Seller Central Desk add-on is the one exception, with its own terms on the products page — you own the account and can revoke access at any time.
