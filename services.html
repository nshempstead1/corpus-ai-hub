Service questions

Why don't you submit for me?

Because the account, the case, and the consequences are yours. Keeping you as the submitter keeps you in control and keeps the process compliant with the platforms' own rules.

What happens if the deadline is missed?

The SLA is part of the product. If we blow it, you get a refund — the storefront for each service states its terms.

Can you guarantee reinstatement / approval / a win?

No, and no honest provider can. We guarantee a complete, consistent, on-time file. Decisions belong to Google, Amazon, tribunals, and reviewers.
