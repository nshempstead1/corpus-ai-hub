How a packet order runs

  • You pay on that SKU’s Stripe Payment Link. The hub has no checkout.
  • You email intake@corpuslocalization.com with the subject tag printed on the thanks page. Attach the notice and the documents. Never send passwords.
  • Deterministic gates run (name/address match, invoice-to-ASIN match, no “Supports” without a test artifact, hearing date ≥3 days out). Fail → hold or gap report, not a invented document.
  • Only Nick releases the file. SLA is delivery of the packet, not Google’s, Amazon’s, a procurement office’s, or a referee’s decision.
