About

One accountable shop, four lines.

Corpus is the public brand of Corpus Localization LLC, a New Jersey company founded on certified translation and built outward from there. Everything we sell follows the same rules: a named price, a named deadline, and a deliverable you can hold us to.

The lines

  • Products — subscription software (Order Desk, Watch Desk, and their add-on desks) that we built, run, and use on our own business first.
  • Growth — landing pages, SEO and AI-search optimization, Google Ads tune-ups, and lead sources for small businesses, sold as fixed scopes.
  • Services — deadline files a platform already asked for: GBP appeal packets, Amazon Plans of Action, VPAT/ACR reports, hearing binders, plus scoped consulting.
  • Localization — certified translations as Corpus Localization, our original line, at corpuslocalization.com.

How we behave

  • We guarantee deliverables and deadlines, never third-party outcomes.
  • Service lines don't take your Google, Amazon, or USCIS logins. You review, you submit.
  • No fabricated documents, no fake reviews, no unbounded retainers.
  • Our own tools monitor our own storefronts — if our checkout breaks, we hear about it in sixty seconds, the same as our customers do.

Who we are not

There are other companies with "Corpus" in the name. We are not corpusai.org (preventive-medicine infrastructure, Bogotá) and not corpusai.io (cybersecurity). We are the New Jersey document-and-software shop legally named Corpus Localization LLC.
